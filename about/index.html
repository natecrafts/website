About Me

I’m Nhat Nguyen, a software engineer specialising in Build Systems, Developer Productivity, and Platform Engineering. Most recently, I spent four and a half years at Oracle working on build infrastructure and security tooling.

I started programming in secondary school with an interest in Pascal and solving algorithmic problems. Later, I found myself studying compilers and tinkering with developer tooling during university, which eventually brought me to the Program Analysis research group and then the Parfait SAST team at Oracle.

Since I started my career as a software engineer, my work has spanned both research and product environments. I have worked on projects in different domains: Cloud Infrastructure and IAM policies, Supply Chain Security, and Build Infrastructure. Having navigated some uniquely challenging problems in these domains, I have developed a confidence to dive into unfamiliar territory and untangle increasingly complex puzzles to unblock myself.

Yet, over time, I realised that my favourite puzzles are the ones that unblock others. These days, I am passionate about coming up with solutions that help engineers work more effectively and feel happier in their daily work. As Russ Cox, an engineer I greatly admire, once said: “Software engineering is what happens to programming when you add time and other programmers.” Developer Productivity and Platform Engineering to me aren’t just about building tools, but also about paying close attention to the pain points of your teammates and actively reducing the friction in our work.

On a human level, I strive to be a good listener, a helpful colleague, and a genuine friend. When I am away from the computer keyboard, I am usually sitting at a musical keyboard or with a guitar. I have a lifelong passion for music, and a very “buggy” version of perfect pitch. (But hey, I usually get the white keys right. I guess that is nothing major, but not minor at all!) I also love following sports, including some less traditional ones like chess and figure skating, where I am drawn to the mix of deep technicality, great perseverance, and the compelling human story behind every athlete.
